The desktop

Aurora is the interface.

A native desktop shell written in C with GTK3 and gtk-layer-shell, running over the labwc Wayland compositor. The shell owns the bar, dock, launcher, wallpaper and Aura panel.

◇

Native, not a webpage

A real desktop shell compiled for the system — not a browser kiosk pretending to be an OS.

▦

Apps & Store

A searchable launcher and Daybreak Store make installing and launching desktop software feel like part of the system.

⌘

Wayland first

labwc provides the compositor layer while Aurora supplies the native desktop experience above it.

On-device intelligence

Meet Aura.

An offline assistant powered by llama.cpp and a bundled Qwen2.5 model. Aura can chat, but it can also control the desktop through the system bridge.

“Ask your computer to do something — and let the system do it.”
open a terminalsystem statusset brightness to 40
Designed for local execution

Your AI lives with your OS.

Common commands can take deterministic fast paths through aurorad, while open-ended requests are handed to the local model. No cloud account is required for the assistant.

The bundled model is deliberately compact and CPU-friendly. Replace the GGUF model if your hardware can support something larger.

Under the surface

Built from the source up.

DaybreakOS follows Linux From Scratch rather than assembling a conventional desktop distribution around an existing environment.
